Tail Eater: Snake Puzzle

A puzzle game for Android

Tail Eater is a calm snake puzzle with one simple rule: snakes eat from the tail. Tap a snake and it crawls straight ahead. If the first thing in its way is the tail of another snake, it eats that snake and grows longer. Bump into a wall, a head or a body and you lose a heart. Clear the board until only one snake is left.
