Paint Drop: Relaxing Coloring

A coloring game for iPhone and Android

Relax with a new kind of coloring: drop paint and watch each picture bloom. Every level is a new picture. Aim the paint bottle and let the drops fly: each one fills an area with color. Finish the picture, add it to your album and go on to the next one.
