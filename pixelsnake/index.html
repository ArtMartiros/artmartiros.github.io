Snakes at the Gate: Color Army

A tower defense game for Android

Snakes at the Gate is a tower defense game about colors. A giant snake of colored cubes crawls toward your gate, and you are the last line of defense. Tap a color to call a shooter to the wall: each shooter hits only the cubes of its own color. Every call is a roll of luck, and a shooter can come out Epic with two barrels or Legendary with three. Break the chests inside the snake, pick cards and grow stronger between runs.
